Fair Play Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Our Commitment to Fair Play

GW eSPORTS is built by gamers, for gamers. We believe that competitive esports must rely entirely on individual skill, strategy, and teamwork. We employ advanced anti-cheat monitoring to ensure that every participant enjoys a level and transparent playing field.

2. Strictly Prohibited Actions

To preserve the competitive integrity of our tournaments, the following actions are strictly forbidden:

  • Using third-party hacks, modded game APKs, aimbots, wallhacks, or macro scripts.
  • Team-ups or collusion between independent squads/players during solo or clash matches.
  • Intentionally exploiting game bugs, glitches, or map flaws to gain unfair advantages.
  • Submitting fake match result screenshots or impersonating other registered players.

3. Consequences of Violations

If our system or admin team flags any player or team for violating our Fair Play standards, immediate actions will be taken, including:

  • Immediate disqualification from ongoing and active tournaments.
  • Permanent suspension and banning of the user account.
  • Forfeiture of all wallet balances, earnings, and unclaimed rewards.

4. Reporting Cheaters

If you encounter suspicious behavior, players using unfair tools, or collusion in any match, you can immediately report them with video evidence to our support team via our official WhatsApp channel for swift investigation.
