Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

At GW eSPORTS, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. We collect information when you register, participate in tournaments, or interact with our support channels:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, phone number, and game user IDs (Free Fire username/UID).
  • Financial Information: UPI IDs or payment handles required for processing instant withdrawals.
  • Technical Data: Device info, IP addresses, and app usage logs for anti-cheat verification.

2. How We Use Your Data

The information we collect is utilized strictly to provide a seamless esports experience, including:

  • Managing your account profile, leaderboards, and tournament matchmaking.
  • Processing prize winnings and fast withdrawal requests securely.
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent activities, multi-accounting, and cheating.
  • Sending critical tournament updates, room credentials, and promotional announcements.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We employ enterprise-grade bank-level encryption (SSL/TLS) to secure your personal data and wallet transactions. Your data is never sold, traded, or rented to external marketing agencies or third parties under any circumstances.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, feel free to reach out to our 24/7 support team via our official WhatsApp channel or in-app live support.
